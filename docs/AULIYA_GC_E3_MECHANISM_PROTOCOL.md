## Decision policy
1. If score-selected ranking substantially improves cross-group versus geometry-selected and true-class alternatives are common, consider candidate/score alignment first; not proof of deployment gain.
2. If assignment deficit concentrates in hard classes across sources, consider targeted supervision. Compare actual class support/scale/geometry, mask quality; endpoint audit cannot establish causality. No loss change solely on low target values.
3. If class predictions are unstable on mild views, consider defect-preserving augmentation/consistency as candidate, with standard augmentation and compute-matched ablation. A stable wrong prediction is not evidence of useful invariance.
4. If true class remains absent across candidate alternatives and views, prioritize discriminative generalization rather than reranking. Select one intervention and test under controlled training; no further open-ended diagnostic sweeps.
E2 HAAR remains optional comparator, not prerequisite. No claim of novelty until comparison with closest literature. One seed, correlated B/C specimens, acquisition/specimen confounding, small eval support. Held-out A locked. No E3 results exist at registration.
