# Thesis Proposal Foundation — v0.15.0

Date: 2026-08-25

Milestone: **section-aware SPs pagination + source-rule correction for figure captions**.

## 1. Pagination rule rechecked against the official guide

The official SPs guide states:

- front matter, from title page through the list of appendices, uses lowercase Roman page numbers centered at the bottom;
- main/end matter from Bab I onward uses Arabic page numbers;
- normal body pages place the number at the top-right;
- pages with a chapter title at the top place the number at the bottom-right;
- page-number position is 3 cm from the right edge and 1.5 cm from the top/bottom edge.

This is now treated as a normative document rule rather than a working-builder preference.

## 2. Section-aware pagination pass added

Created:

`tools/thesis/apply_sps_pagination.py`

The pass:

1. inserts a next-page section break before each `BAB ...` Heading 1;
2. creates one front-matter section plus one section for each chapter;
3. normalizes every section to A4 and official 4/3/3/3 cm margins;
4. sets header/footer distance to 1.5 cm;
5. uses lowercase Roman numbering centered in the front-matter footer;
6. restarts Arabic numbering at 1 for Bab I;
7. continues Arabic numbering through later chapters;
8. uses a top-right header on normal body pages;
9. uses a bottom-right first-page footer on chapter-opening pages.

The workflow now runs this pagination pass after figure insertion and before PDF rendering.

## 3. Pagination implementation tested on a synthetic DOCX

Before committing the script, the section-break/page-number logic was tested in the local document environment with:

- a Roman-numbered front section;
- multiple chapter sections;
- `different first page` behavior;
- a long multi-page chapter.

The synthetic render confirmed:

- front page rendered with Roman `i` centered at the bottom;
- Bab I opening page rendered Arabic `1` at the bottom-right;
- following Bab I page rendered Arabic `2` at the top-right;
- section audit showed A4 with margins corresponding to left 4 cm and top/right/bottom 3 cm.

This validates the pagination mechanism itself. It does **not** substitute for rendering the actual proposal; full-proposal pagination and page-break QA remain required.

## 4. Figure-caption punctuation source boundary corrected

The earlier assembly QA over-stated that the official guide explicitly prohibited a period after the **figure number**. Re-reading the guide shows that it requires:

- title case;
- caption below and symmetric with the figure;
- no terminal period.

The supplied Word scaffold explicitly demonstrates `Gambar 2.1. Grafik X`. Therefore the working proposal now follows the scaffold separator convention:

`Gambar 3.1. Kerangka Penelitian`

while retaining TNR 12, centered placement, and no terminal period.

For tables, the rule is different and explicit: the official guide states that no period is used after the table number. The v2 builder therefore retains:

`Tabel 2.1  Perbandingan penelitian terkait`
